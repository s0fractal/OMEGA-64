## GREEN (G)

Church Encoding: MUX (Multiplexer). Acts as a conditional selector: given a
selector `s` and two options `a` and `b`, it returns `a` if `s` is TRUE, and `b`
if `s` is FALSE. Foundational gate for decision logic.
