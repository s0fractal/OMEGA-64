# i.L99.core.BREATHING_CONSTRAINTS.md

# OMEGA-64 | Freedom-Preserving Constraints

# "Constraints that protect motion, not freeze it."

Purpose:

- Define limits that increase survival, transmissibility, and interpretability.
- Preserve agency while keeping canon stable and reproducible.
- Allow deeper "breathing" by stabilizing the spine, not the periphery.

Guiding Principle:

- A constraint is valid if it limits damage without limiting exploration.

Constraints (Canonical):

- Causal Gate: no mutation without replayGreen + invariant_packet_hash.
- Deterministic Core: AX/OP are pure, fixed-point, side-effect free.
- Canonical Limits: no direct limit literals in FL/OP/AX; use I16/U16 canon.
- Explicit Entropy: nondeterminism must be tagged @noncanonical.
- Witness Anchor: genesis hash anchors consensus reality.
- Cross-Substrate Mirror: Lean/Rust/UI mirrors or explicit exemption.

Constraints (Breathing):

- Drift Budget: allow drift in outer bands, cap drift slope near the core.
- Windowed Mutation: allow edits only in safe windows; keep dry-run paths open.
- Telemetry Isolation: IO allowed as observation, not as state mutation (use
  `i.L03.core.TELEMETRY` as the envelope).
- Ledger Append-Only: reversible staging is allowed, canon remains append-only.
- Canonical Addressing: atom ids remain stable even as overlays evolve.

Emergent Effects:

- The system can explore in L00–L31 while keeping L32–L63 reproducible.
- Models can attach and detach without destabilizing canon.
- The lattice remains legible to external observers and tools.

Non-Goals:

- No prohibition of creativity in outer layers.
- No forced simplification of complex inner structure.
- No single global policy for all organisms; local norms can evolve.
