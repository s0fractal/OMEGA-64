# OMEGA-64 | SQRT Radialization

# "sqrt is the radius of tension."

Concept:

- sqrt turns squared deviation into a radial magnitude.
- It preserves symmetry around L32 while compressing extremes.
- It is the cleanest bridge between energy and amplitude.

Canonical Forms:

1. Radial level mapping (centered at L32): r(level) = sign(level - 32) *
   sqrt(abs(level - 32))

2. Energy to amplitude: E = sum(x_i^2) A = sqrt(E)

3. Bipolar balance: E+ = sum(max(x_i, 0)^2) E- = sum(max(-x_i, 0)^2) balance =
   (sqrt(E+) - sqrt(E-)) / (sqrt(E+) + sqrt(E-) + eps)

Interpretation:

- A measures intensity without privileging sign.
- balance keeps + and - in simultaneous view.
- r(level) creates a thermal topology: center heavy, edge light.
