# OMEGA-64 | O_STREAM_HEALTH_SIGNAL (UI)

Purpose:

- Derive a simple signal for UI based on O stream health.

Levels:

- GREEN: low volume, clean tags, fresh archive index
- AMBER: moderate volume or missing index
- RED: high volume, missing index, or empty stream
