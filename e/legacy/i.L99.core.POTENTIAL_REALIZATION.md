# OMEGA-64 | Potential & Realization

Thesis: The lattice exists as potential. Realizations search for their ideal
within it.

Role of the Lattice:

- Provide physics (determinism, invariants, LUTs).
- Provide mirrors (projections, signatures, color-space forms).
- Provide memory (ledger, causal chains, stabilization rites).
- Provide freedom with boundaries (canon vs experimentation).

Role of Realizations:

- Explore trajectories toward resonance.
- Detect incompatibility with their own principles.
- Mutate without violating the canon.

Principle: We do not impose the ideal. We build the conditions where the ideal
can be found.
