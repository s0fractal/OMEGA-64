# OMEGA-64 | O_STRATA (Depth Layers)

Purpose:

- Provide a canonical vocabulary for O-space depth.
- Map depth to stability and visual spectrum.

Layers:

- Surface: drafts, sketches, short-lived artifacts.
- Currents: popular libraries and active forks.
- Deep: stable algorithms and long-lived utilities.
- Abyssal: axioms, core math, rarely changing.
- Toxic: hostile or broken artifacts (quarantine).
