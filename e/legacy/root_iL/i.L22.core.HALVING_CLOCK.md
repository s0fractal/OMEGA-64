# OMEGA-64 | HALVING_CLOCK (Ritual Rhythm)

Purpose:

- Define a halving-based rhythm as an optional time driver.

Intent:

- Halving epochs act as a pulse for CHRONOFLUX tuning and crystallization
  rituals.
- This is a mode, not a canon law.

Guidance:

- The rhythm must be parameterized (no hard-coded BTC dependence).
- Operators may map external epochs (e.g., BTC halving) to internal ticks.
- The halving clock can be disabled without invalidating canon.

Inputs (conceptual):

- epoch_index
- epoch_length_ticks
- anchor_tick (genesis reference)

Outputs (conceptual):

- phase_shift
- flow_rate_adjustment
- ritual_window_hint
